## How to choose
1. Ask your supervisor which journals the department recognises (Scopus, Web of Science, UGC-CARE as required by Brainware).
2. Confirm indexing on the Scopus Sources list and the UGC-CARE list yourself. Do not rely on third-party sites that list impact factors; several appeared in my search and some are unreliable.
3. Avoid predatory journals: check that the journal is on the publisher's own site, has an ISSN, named editors, and a transparent fee page.
4. Match evidence to venue:
   - Simulation only: a conference or workshop paper, or a clearly labelled methods or preprint paper.
   - Real pilot cohort with faculty ratings and a baseline comparison: IEEE Access or Education and Information Technologies.
   - Real cohort plus a methodological advance that beats the baselines: Applied Soft Computing.
5. Before the final choice, read the journal's author guidelines page for word limit, structure, reference style, ethics and data statements, and similarity-check policy.
